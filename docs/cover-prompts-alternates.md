# FORM / FIELD cover prompts: alternate versions

Second versions of some style prompts. The site reads only `docs/cover-prompts.md`, one prompt per style. To make one of these the live prompt for a style, swap it into that file. Each prompt's `Cover` line names the image it made; the site shows these prompts beside their similar covers.

## Italo Disco: beach compilation sleeve

- Slug: `italo-disco`
- Artwork: 1985 dance compilation LP cover (Airbrush on board)
- Format: square 1:1
- Cover: `public/covers/italo-disco1.png`

```text
The original front cover of a 1985 Italo disco dance compilation LP, painted in airbrush and gouache on illustration board.

Composition: a tropical beach at dusk seen from low on the sand. Two tall palm trees lean in from the left, their fronds silhouetted against the sky, and a bright sun flare bursts through them with long diagonal light rays. The sky shifts from sunset gold at the horizon to deep space navy at the top, scattered with stars, a thin crescent moon and a small ringed planet, so the scene feels half holiday and half outer space. A calm turquoise sea stretches to the horizon. In the lower right, cropped at the chest, a glamorous woman with dark wavy hair, a red silk headscarf, dark sunglasses and a silver metallic strapless top turns her face up toward the light. A glowing pink neon script logo sweeps across the upper right.

Typography: a flowing, hand-drawn neon script in hot pink with a white inner glow for the title, and a small sleek italic sans-serif line in chrome. Set exactly this text: "costa neon" as the script logo; "Dance Hits Vol. 2" small beneath it.

Colours: space navy #0D0A24 at the top of the sky, laser pink #E83FB4 in the logo and the edge of the sky, chrome blue #8FD3FF in the sea and on the metallic top, spotlight gold #FFD86B in the sun and horizon, with turquoise water, warm sand and a touch of red in the headscarf.

Light: a strong low sun flare behind the palms, warm rim light on the woman's face and shoulders, and chrome glints on her top and sunglasses.

Finish: hand-airbrushed acrylic on board: smooth sprayed skies, crisp masked edges on the palms and the logo, glossy airbrushed skin tones, four-point star glints flicked in white paint and faint board texture. Glamorous, synthetic and made for dancing, not a photograph or a 3D render.

Format: square (1:1). Show the flat sleeve artwork itself, edge to edge, not a vinyl mockup.

Text: spell the lettering exactly as written; add no other words, record-label logos, stickers or signatures.

Avoid: the likeness of a real person, daylight realism, paper texture, earth-toned grading, modern 3D rendering, copying any existing album cover.
```

**If it misses:** Make it look hand-airbrushed: smoother sprayed sky, crisper palm silhouettes and glossy airbrushed skin. Correct the logo to read exactly "costa neon" and "Dance Hits Vol. 2".

## Maximalism: bright muralled living room

- Slug: `maximalism`
- Artwork: Interior photograph (Design-magazine editorial)
- Format: tall 2:3
- Cover: `public/covers/maximalism2.png`

```text
An editorial interior photograph for a design magazine: a joyful maximalist living room, shot with a wide lens.

Composition: a view into the corner of the room. The left wall is bubblegum pink, with an ornate white fireplace, a mirror framed in painted flowers and a vase of pink peonies on the mantel. The right wall is deep cobalt blue, painted with a huge mural of oversized tropical flowers and leaves in yellow, orange and green, with an orange archway leading into the next room. In front are two curvy mustard-yellow armchairs, a zebra-striped side chair, a sculptural coffee table in swirled pastel resin topped with flowers and books, a black-and-white striped footstool and large potted monstera plants. The floor is covered by a bold black, white, red and yellow abstract-pattern rug. Every surface has colour or pattern, but the room is carefully arranged and balanced around the coffee table.

Colours: bubblegum pink and cobalt blue walls, saffron yellow #E3A72F, raspberry #B0265B, bottle green #1F3B2D in the plants, tangerine orange, and black-and-white patterns.

Light: bright, soft daylight from a window out of frame, with gentle natural shadows and true, saturated colour.

Finish: a real photograph of a real room: fabric texture on the upholstery, visible brushstrokes in the mural, small imperfections and natural depth, not a 3D render.

Format: tall portrait (2:3). Show the photograph itself, full frame.

Text: no lettering, readable book titles, logos or watermarks anywhere.

Avoid: empty space, beige or muted palettes, sterile minimalism, CGI-looking furniture, clutter with no arrangement.
```

## Glitch: dissolving portrait

- Slug: `glitch`
- Artwork: Glitch-art portrait (Archival pigment print)
- Format: tall 2:3
- Cover: `public/covers/glitch1.png`

```text
An original contemporary glitch-art portrait, printed as a large archival pigment print for a gallery.

Composition: the head and shoulders of an invented young woman, seen from the front and slightly off-centre, gazing calmly past the viewer. Her face and hair dissolve sideways into long horizontal smears and streaks, as if the image were dragged across the screen. Bands of coral-red and teal paint-like distortion sweep across her forehead and hair and trail off toward the right edge, while her eyes, nose and lips stay clear and recognisable. Thin horizontal displacement lines slice through the lower face and shoulders, shifting small slices sideways. The red and cyan channels split apart along her outline. Behind her, the background is a pale off-white on one side and deep bottle-green shadow on the other, with a few scattered blocks of compressed pixels. Calm expression, violent distortion: the contrast is the point.

Colours: coral red and orange, the red channel #FF0040 in the strongest splits, teal and the cyan channel #00FFD1, deep green-black shadows close to dead pixel black #0B0B0D, and blown white #F2F2F2 in the brightest highlights and the pale background.

Light: soft, even studio light on the face, with blown-out highlights where the streaks are brightest.

Finish: a blend of photographic skin detail and painterly digital smearing: long pixel-drag streaks, crisp horizontal tears, clean RGB offsets, a little compression blockiness and fine grain. It looks made by hand in software, not random noise.

Format: tall portrait (2:3). Show the print itself, edge to edge, with no wall, frame or mockup.

Text: no lettering, logos, watermarks or signatures anywhere.

Avoid: the likeness of a real person, a fully destroyed or unreadable face, muddy colour, soft pastels, horror styling, copying any existing image.
```

**If it misses:** Keep the eyes, nose and lips clear and sharp, and move the streaks and smears to the hair, forehead and edges. Keep the colours.

## Surveillance: car-park security-camera still

- Slug: `surveillance`
- Artwork: 1997 CCTV still (Video frame, exhibited as a gallery print)
- Format: square 1:1
- Cover: not yet

```text
A single frame from 1997 security-camera footage, exhibited as a large print in a contemporary photography show.

Composition: a fixed, high-angle wide shot from a camera mounted in the ceiling corner of an empty underground car park at night, with strong fisheye distortion. Concrete pillars, painted bay lines, one parked car and a single figure in a hooded jacket crossing the floor, slightly blurred mid-step. The camera looks down, detached and still.

Typography: a blocky white monospace video overlay. Set exactly this text: "CAM 04" in the top left; "09-28-1997 03:12:44" in the bottom right; "REC" in the top right beside a small red dot.

Colours: monitor dark #1C221E, night-vision green #7F9A84, washed grey #C7CCC6, record red #E94B3C for the dot only.

Light: flat, flickering fluorescent overhead light mixed with infrared night vision.

Finish: low-resolution sensor noise, interlacing lines, compression blocks, slight smearing and a desaturated green-grey cast: a real frame of old CCTV tape.

Format: Square (1:1). Show the video frame itself, full frame, not a monitor.

Text: spell the overlay exactly as written; add no other words, logos or watermarks.

Avoid: cinematic shallow depth of field, vivid colour, flattering lighting, sharp detail.
```

**If it misses:** Make it look more like old CCTV tape: more sensor noise, interlacing and compression blocks, and a flatter green-grey tone. Keep the overlay text exactly as it is.
