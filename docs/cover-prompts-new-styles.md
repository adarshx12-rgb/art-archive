## Baroque & Tenebrism: scholar reading by a shaft of light

- Suggested slug: `baroque-tenebrism`
- Artwork: Early-1600s Baroque oil painting (Oil on canvas)
- Format: tall 2:3
- Cover: `covers-src/_new-styles/baroque-tenebrism.png`
- Note: the subject is deliberately secular, so the image doesn't invent new pictures of sacred figures or copy existing masterpieces.

```text
An original oil painting on canvas in the Baroque style of the early 1600s, using tenebrism: most of the picture is lost in deep shadow, and a single strong beam of light picks out the figures, as in the great old-master paintings of that time.

Composition: a vast, dark stone hall with tall carved columns and arches that dissolve into blackness above. From a high window on the upper left, one shaft of warm light falls diagonally across the floor and lands on a small group in the lower centre: an elderly scholar with a long grey beard, in a deep crimson robe, reading aloud from a large open manuscript that rests on a wooden table, while a young apprentice in a cream linen shirt leans in to listen and a woman in a plain ochre dress holds a small oil lamp beside them. A few more listeners stand half-seen at the edge of the light, their faces only just catching the glow. Everything outside the beam fades into warm, velvety darkness. Calm, thoughtful and reverent in mood, with the lit faces and the open book as the clear focal point.

Colours: deep warm blacks and dark umber in the shadows, crimson and madder red in the robe, ochre and golden brown, cream and warm ivory in the lit linen and skin, and small touches of olive green.

Light: tenebrism: one strong, directional light source from the upper left, with dramatic falloff into near-total darkness, sculpted faces and hands, bright highlights on cloth folds, and soft reflected light in the half-shadows.

Finish: a real old-master oil painting: rich layered glazes, thick impasto in the brightest highlights, softly blended flesh tones, heavy folds of drapery painted with care, visible canvas weave, fine craquelure and a slightly aged, warm varnish tone. Photographed evenly for a museum collection.

Format: tall portrait (2:3). Show it as a flat museum photograph of the whole canvas, cropped exactly to its edges, with no wall, frame or gallery.

Text: no lettering, inscriptions, signatures, logos or watermarks anywhere.

Avoid: religious figures, saints, halos or sacred symbols; nudity; gore or violence; copying any existing painting; modern clothing or objects; bright, even lighting; a smooth digital or CGI look.
```

**If it misses:** Make it darker and more like a real old-master painting: deeper shadows, one strong beam of light, richer glazes and visible canvas texture. Keep the scene and figures the same.

## Dreamy Motion Blur: spinning dancer

- Suggested slug: `dreamy-motion-blur` (close to the trending "Grainy Blur" look)
- Artwork: Experimental art photograph (Long-exposure, heavy film grain)
- Format: tall 2:3
- Cover: not yet

```text
An original experimental art photograph: a dancer spinning mid-leap in a dark studio, shot with a long exposure while the camera moved, so the figure smears into dreamy ribbons of light. Printed as a large, grainy gallery print.

Composition: the dancer turns diagonally across the centre of the frame, arms flung out, a long silk scarf and loose silver fabric sweeping around the body. The figure stretches and twists sideways into soft, flowing ghost trails, with two or three faint repeated outlines of the head, arms and shoulders overlapping like a double exposure. A curved pool of electric-blue stage light glows across the floor in the lower left corner. Warm amber light trails from the moving fabric swirl around the torso and legs like ribbons, and a few thin, sharp streaks of light cut across the frame. The rest is deep, soft darkness. Dreamlike and weightless: the figure is still readable, but everything feels in motion.

Colours: deep plum-black background, electric sky blue in the stage light, warm amber and orange light trails, and silver-white highlights on the fabric and skin.

Light: a single spotlight and the blue floor light, with glowing highlights that bloom and bleed into the dark and soft light wrapping around the smeared shapes.

Finish: heavy, visible film grain and fine noise across the whole image, long-exposure motion smear, soft ghosting, slight warping as if the image were stretched, and gentle halation around the brightest areas. It looks like real analog photography pushed to its limits, not a clean digital render.

Format: tall portrait (2:3). Show the photograph itself, full frame.

Text: no lettering, interface elements, logos or watermarks anywhere.

Avoid: sharp, frozen motion, clean CGI, the likeness of a real person, cluttered backgrounds, flat even lighting.
```

**If it misses:** Make it dreamier: stronger motion smear and ghosting, heavier film grain, and more glow bleeding from the highlights. Keep the figure just readable.
